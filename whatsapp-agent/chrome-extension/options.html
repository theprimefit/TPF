Hindivine Leads

Connect the extension to your WhatsApp lead agent.

What you get

  • Desktop alerts for new leads, payments to verify, chats that need a person and hot leads.
  • A red count on the toolbar icon, and a popup with one-click Mark paid and Resume bot.
  • On web.whatsapp.com, a lead card next to the open chat with quick replies you can check before sending.

If the agent runs on this computer, use http://localhost:3000. Chrome will ask once to allow access to that address.
